# TandemForge artwork

The cover and pairing illustration were created with Codex's built-in ImageGen. The application uses compressed WebP copies.

| File | Generation brief |
| --- | --- |
| [Cover](../dist/cover.webp) | Wide editorial 3D still life with a graphite mini PC and a phone linked by mint light, brushed metal rings and a translucent lens. Dark petrol backdrop with space at left for live copy. |
| [Pairing illustration](../dist/pairing.webp) | A phone hovering over a graphite dock with a mint ring and abstract glass tiles. Industrial studio lighting with graphite, mint and warm ivory. |

The TandemForge app mark is an editable [SVG](../dist/mark.svg). The two diagonal bands refer to the desktop and phone working together.

## ImageGen prompts

### Cover

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: wide cover for an open-source local AI studio for desktop and phone. Generate a polished editorial 3D illustration, landscape 1536x1024. A graphite mini desktop computer and an upright thin modern smartphone form a single still life on the RIGHT half, linked by two thin luminous mint-green strands. The phone glass shows only abstract mint interface blocks, no readable marks. A few precise metallic rings and one luminous translucent mint lens add a feeling of creative tools. Restrained palette: almost-black petrol green #101c19, graphite, cool silver, mint #a9e5ca, a tiny warm cream highlight. Low-key studio lighting, realistic materials, clean shadows, excellent silhouette. Leave the left third nearly empty dark petrol for live typesetting, and keep both objects within center-safe bounds so the image also crops wide. Premium industrial design publication, intentionally quiet. No letters, no logos, no watermark, no humans.

### Pairing illustration

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: small supporting illustration for a private Windows-to-phone pairing panel in an open-source local AI studio. Landscape 1536x1024 composition. Close-up single phone at a slight angle floating above a graphite docking tile; a luminous mint ring and a few precisely placed glass squares visually suggest pairing and a QR, but absolutely no actual QR code or readable characters. Center the subject so it works cropped into a small wide tile. Dark petrol green near #101c19, brushed graphite and mint #a9e5ca, warm cream highlight. Understated editorial industrial-design render, softly lit, clean negative space. No text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
